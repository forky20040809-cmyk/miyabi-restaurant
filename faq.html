Frequently Asked Questions

請於用餐日前48小時來電或以電子郵件通知我們。逾時取消或無故未到，將酌收每人 NT$500 訂位保證金。
Please cancel or modify at least 48 hours in advance. Late cancellations may incur a NT$500 per person fee.

我們可以配合素食、無麩質、海鮮過敏等特殊飲食需求，請於訂位時在「特殊需求」欄位填寫。
We accommodate vegetarian, gluten-free, and allergy-related needs. Please note them when booking.

無菜單料理需至少提前 7個工作天 預訂。主廚餐桌體驗每日限2席，建議提前2週以上預訂。
Omakase requires 7 business days' advance booking. Chef's Table is limited to 2 seats daily.

餐廳本身無專屬停車場，建議搭乘大眾運輸工具。附近有仁愛路停車場（步行約5分鐘），或搭捷運至忠孝敦化站步行約8分鐘。
No dedicated parking. Nearest MRT: Zhongxiao Dunhua Station (8 min walk).

歡迎攜帶外部蛋糕，酌收 NT$300 開瓶費（含餐具與蠟燭服務）。
Outside cakes are welcome with a NT$300 cakeage fee (includes service, plates and candles).

我們建議穿著 Smart Casual 以上的服裝，請避免穿著拖鞋、短褲或運動服前來。
Smart casual or above is recommended. Flip-flops, shorts and athletic wear are not permitted.

我們接受現金、所有主要信用卡（Visa、Mastercard、JCB、American Express）、LINE Pay 及街口支付。
We accept cash, all major credit cards, LINE Pay and Jkopay.

可以，我們提供電子發票及紙本收據。如需公司抬頭統編，請於結帳前告知服務人員。
Yes. For company invoices (GUI number), please inform your server before payment.
