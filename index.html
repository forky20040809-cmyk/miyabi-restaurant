關於雅 · About Us

料理即是藝術

Cuisine as a Form of Art

雅 Miyabi 由主廚林宸佑於2018年創立。林宸佑自幼熱愛料理，二十歲負笈日本，先後於京都懷石名店「瓢亭」及東京米其林三星割烹習藝長達十五年，深刻體會「一期一會」的精神。返台後，他決心以台灣豐沛的在地食材結合道地日本技藝，打造這片屬於台灣的料理美學空間。

Chef Lin Cheng-Yu honed his craft over 15 years in Japan — at the legendary Hyotei in Kyoto and a three-Michelin-star kappo in Tokyo. Returning to Taiwan, he founded Miyabi to bridge the finest Japanese culinary traditions with the island's exceptional local produce.

主廚介紹 · Meet the Chef

林宸佑

Chef Lin Cheng-Yu

1985年生於台南，林宸佑自高中起便深受日本飲食文化吸引。19歲隻身赴日，初入京都老舖磨練基本功，從洗碗、備料做起，五年後獲得師父認可，升任板前主廚助理。

2008年轉赴東京，加入米其林三星割烹「銀座 木村」，在嚴格的日式廚房文化中淬鍊技藝，精通懷石料理各道工序，並深研高湯萃取、刀工美學與食材時令哲學。

Born in Tainan in 1985, Chef Lin left for Japan at 19 with nothing but passion. After fifteen years mastering kaiseki at Kyoto's Hyotei and a Michelin three-star kappo in Ginza, he returned home to open Miyabi — a restaurant that marries Japanese technique with the abundance of Taiwan's land and sea.

Please cancel or modify at least 48 hours in advance by phone or email. Late cancellations or no-shows may incur a NT$500 per person fee.

我們可以配合素食、無麩質、海鮮過敏等特殊飲食需求，請於訂位時在「特殊需求」欄位填寫，或提前來電告知，主廚將為您特別設計料理。

We accommodate vegetarian, gluten-free, and allergy-related dietary needs. Please note them when booking and our chef will customize accordingly.

Omakase requires at least 7 business days' advance booking. Chef's Table is limited to 2 seats daily — we recommend booking 2+ weeks ahead.

餐廳本身無專屬停車場，建議搭乘大眾運輸工具前來。附近有仁愛路停車場（步行約5分鐘），或搭乘捷運至忠孝敦化站步行約8分鐘可抵達。

No dedicated parking. We recommend public transport. Nearest MRT: Zhongxiao Dunhua Station (8 min walk). Nearby parking available on Ren'ai Road.

Outside cakes are welcome with a NT$300 cakeage fee (includes service, plates and candles). Our Celebration Set already includes a bespoke dessert.
